Make your own palette

Every tower in Box Stack climbs through bands of colour. Pick a background and three to ten crate colours, by hand or from a picture, and try them on the real game: drag a crate from the hand to see it on the crane. Then add it to the vote, where everyone can give it a thumbs up or down

What this page saves

Your votes and the palettes you make, with a random ID your browser makes up so each person counts once. No name, no account, no cookies, and your IP address isn't stored. It's kept on Cloudflare and only used to pick Box Stack's colours. Your browser remembers which palettes you've seen, on your device only. A picture you take colours from never leaves your device
