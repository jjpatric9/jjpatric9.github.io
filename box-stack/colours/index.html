Help pick Box Stack's colours

Every tower in Box Stack climbs through bands of colour, and I'd love your help choosing them. Each palette is shown on the real game, so you see it the way you'd play it. Drag a crate from the hand to see it on the crane, over its landing outline and on the tower.

  • In the game goes through every palette in the game now, one at a time, and never the same one twice. Keep it, or vote to take it out
  • New palettes are ones other players made. Yes if you'd like it in the game
  • Make your own from scratch, or take colours from any picture with the eyedropper, then add it to the vote

On a keyboard: → keep or yes, ← remove or no, Z undo

What this page saves

Your votes, the palettes you make and the ones you'd take out, along with a random ID your browser makes up so each person only counts once. No name, no account, no cookies, and your IP address isn't stored. It's kept on Cloudflare and only ever used to pick Box Stack's colours. Your browser also remembers what you've already seen, on your device only, so you don't get the same palette twice. A picture you take colours from stays on your device: it is never uploaded or saved.
