Every tower in Box Stack climbs through bands of colour. Here is every palette there is, one at a time, each played live on the real game

  • Swipe up for the next palette, or scroll
  • Thumbs up if you want it in the game, thumbs down if you don't. Tap it again to take it back
  • In the game means Box Stack has it now. New ones were made by players
  • + makes your own, by hand or from a picture

On a keyboard: ↓ next, ↑ back, → thumbs up, ← thumbs down

What this page saves

Your votes and the palettes you make, with a random ID your browser makes up so each person counts once. No name, no account, no cookies, and your IP address isn't kept. It's kept on Cloudflare and only used to pick Box Stack's colours. The number of thumbs up and down on each palette is shown to everyone, but never who gave them. Your browser remembers your votes, on your device only. A picture you take colours from never leaves your device
