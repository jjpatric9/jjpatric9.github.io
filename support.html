FAQ

Is Box Stack free?

Yes, with ads.

Is it on iPhone?

Yes, on the App Store

Why don't finished rows disappear?

They set and become the new floor. Rows below stop counting toward your lean.

My tower fell over, but it looked balanced!

Boxes up high pull harder than boxes down low. Watch the bar.

Can I rotate the boxes?

No. Every hand can still be survived.

Does the game need the internet?

Only for ads. Nothing you build leaves your phone. See the privacy policy.

How do I delete my data?

Clear the app's data or delete the app. Everything is stored on your device.

Can I suggest something?

Please do! Email me or leave a review.
