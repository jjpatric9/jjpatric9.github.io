Reporting a bug

These details make a problem much faster to track down:

  • Your phone model and Android version
  • The build number, shown at the bottom of the game-over panel (like build 0.9.42.a1b2c3d)
  • What you were doing when it happened, and what you expected instead
  • A screenshot or screen recording, if you can catch it

Common questions

Why doesn't a completed row disappear?

That's deliberate — BoxStacker isn't a falling-block puzzle. A completed row sets: it stays exactly where it is and becomes your tower's new foundation, which re-centres the balance and gives you the full width to build on. Completing rows is how you keep going, but nothing ever compacts down.

My tower fell over and it looked balanced.

Height is not what kills you — lopsidedness is. Crates near the top of the tower pull far harder than crates near the bottom, so a small overhang high up matters more than a big one low down. The gauge shows the risk, not just the lean; when it turns red, the next hand is chosen to give you a way out.

Can I rotate pieces?

No — a crate is placed exactly as it's dealt. That's the constraint the whole game is built around, and the hand you're given is checked to be survivable before you're dealt it.

How do I delete my data?

Clear the app's data in Android settings, or uninstall the app. Everything BoxStacker saves is on your device only — see the privacy policy.

Does the game need internet?

No. BoxStacker requests no permissions at all, including no internet permission. It works entirely offline and cannot send anything anywhere.

Accessibility

BoxStacker is playable with a screen reader, and colour is never used to convey information you need. Reduced motion and high-contrast crates can both be turned on in settings. If something about it doesn't work for you, please tell me — that's the kind of report I most want.
