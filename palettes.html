What this page saves

Your votes, the palettes you make and the ones you'd take out, along with a random ID your browser makes up so each person only counts once. No name, no account, no cookies, and your IP address isn't stored. It's kept on Cloudflare and only ever used to pick Box Stack's colours. Your browser also remembers what you've already seen, on your device only, so you don't get the same palette twice.
