What this page saves

Your votes, your palettes and the ones you mark, with a random ID your browser makes up so that each person counts once. No name, no account, no cookies, and your IP address isn't stored. It's kept with Cloudflare, used only to choose BoxStacker's colours, and never sold or shared. Your browser also remembers what you've already seen, on your device only, so you aren't shown it twice.
