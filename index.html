Box Stack

Build. Balance. Repeat.

A crane hands you boxes and you stack them on a little boat. Fill a row all the way across and it locks in, giving you solid ground to build on.

The catch is balance. Every box pulls the tower toward its own side, and the higher it sits, the harder it pulls. Get too lopsided and the whole thing rolls over into the harbour. It's very much a “just one more tower” kind of game.

Every tower you build gets measured against a real landmark, from Stonehenge all the way up to the Burj Khalifa. The CN Tower is in there too, obviously.

Get it free on Google Play How to play and tips
  • No timers, no lives, no energyPlay at your own pace. One tower, for as long as you can keep it standing.
  • Plays offlineNo account and no sign-in. If you get interrupted, it remembers your run.
  • A new look every runEach tower climbs through its own set of colours, and you can help pick them.
  • Something to brag aboutBadges to earn, landmarks to pass, and a Brag Book that keeps your records.
  • Works with TalkBackThe board and the boxes describe themselves, and every piece can be placed with a screen reader.

How I make games

A little obsessive, in a good way

I've been writing software for six years, and these days I'm putting all of that into games. I use AI tools to help me build, and then I test everything like crazy, because the stuff you never notice is usually what makes a game feel good.

Played by bots first

Before an update goes out, bots play thousands of complete games against the rules to make sure nothing's broken.

Tough, but fair

Every set of boxes is picked by looking at your actual tower and checked to make sure there's a way to survive it. Finding it is up to you.

Your stuff stays yours

No account, no sign-in, and nothing you build ever leaves your phone. Ads from Google AdMob keep the game free, and Tenjin tells me which of my ads are working. The privacy policy has the details.

Box Stack is my first game. It won't be my last.
