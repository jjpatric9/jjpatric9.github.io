Privacy Policy

For BoxStacker (com.boxstacker.app), published by TallmanGames.

Last updated: 14 August 2026

In short: BoxStacker collects nothing about you. It has no network access, no analytics, no advertising, no accounts and no tracking of any kind. Everything it saves stays on your device.

Who is responsible

BoxStacker is made and published by TallmanGames, a sole developer operation run by Joshua Patrick in Canada. Questions about this policy or about your privacy can be sent to jjpatric9@gmail.com.

Information the app collects

None. BoxStacker does not collect, transmit, sell or share any personal information. It does not ask for your name, email address, location or any account, and it contains no advertising, analytics or crash-reporting software.

BoxStacker requests no Android permissions at all — including no internet permission. The app is technically incapable of sending information off your device. This is a property of how it is built, not only a promise about how it is operated.

Information stored on your device

So the game works between sessions, BoxStacker saves the following locally on your phone:

  • Your best score, best tower height and best row count
  • How many games you have played, and totals for rows and pieces
  • Your recent scores
  • Your settings — sound, ambience, haptics, reduced motion, high-contrast crates
  • Whether you have seen the tutorial
  • A snapshot of a game in progress, so an interrupted game can be resumed

This information never leaves your device, is not linked to you or to any identifier, and is not accessible to TallmanGames or to anyone else.

Android backup

If you have Android's backup feature enabled, your scores and settings may be included in your device's backup to your own Google account, in the same way as other apps on your phone. This is handled entirely by Android and is controlled by your device settings — the developer has no access to it. Your saved game in progress is deliberately excluded from backup.

Children

BoxStacker is suitable for all ages. Because it collects no information whatsoever, it collects nothing from children either, and it complies with COPPA and similar rules by simply not gathering data.

Your data rights

Because no data ever reaches the developer, there is nothing held about you to access, correct, export or delete. To remove everything BoxStacker has stored on your own device, either clear the app's data through Android's app settings or uninstall the app.

Third parties

BoxStacker contains no third-party advertising, analytics or tracking software, and shares no data with anyone. The app is distributed through Google Play; Google's own handling of your Play account and downloads is covered by Google's Privacy Policy and is outside the developer's control.

Changes to this policy

If a future version of BoxStacker introduces anything that collects or transmits data — such as advertising — this policy will be updated before that version is released, and the Google Play Data safety information will be updated to match. The date at the top of this page shows when it was last changed.
