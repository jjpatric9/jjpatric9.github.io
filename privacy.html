Privacy Policy — Box Stack

For Box Stack (com.boxstacker.app), developed and published by Tallman Games.

Effective 23 September 2026

This policy describes what the app stores, what its advertising and attribution providers collect, and the choices available to you.

The app has no account, no sign-in and no server of ours. Nothing you build, score or unlock is transmitted to us. It contains no crash-reporting software. The app uses the network to display adverts and measure which advertising campaigns lead to installs and returning sessions.

1. Information stored on your device

The app stores the following locally and does not transmit it:

  • your scores, records, badges, landmarks and experience total
  • lifetime counts of runs, rows, boxes and time played
  • your settings, and whether you have completed the tutorial
  • the game in progress
  • when an advert was last shown, and how many runs ago, so that adverts can be spaced

This information is not linked to your identity, and we have no means of accessing it.

2. Information collected for advertising and attribution

Adverts are supplied by Google AdMob. To serve them, Google collects and processes:

  • your device's advertising identifier, a resettable ID that does not contain your name, email address or telephone number
  • app activity: that an advert was requested, displayed or interacted with
  • device and network information inherent in making the request, including IP address

Google acts as an independent data controller for this information. Its use is governed by Google's Privacy Policy and by how Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services. We do not receive the underlying identifiers or advertising profiles from Google.

Adverts appear as a banner, occasionally between runs, and optionally when you choose to watch one to continue a run. No advert is shown while a run is in progress.

Tenjin measures whether an install or later app opening resulted from an advertising campaign. For that purpose its software may process your advertising identifier, an app-specific installation identifier, install-referrer and campaign information, IP address, device and operating-system information, language, locale and country, network information, app version, and app-open and session timing. It does not receive your scores, towers, records, badges, landmarks, settings or other gameplay.

Tenjin processes this information for us and may send campaign attribution to the advertising network responsible for the campaign, including AppLovin. Tenjin uses infrastructure in the United States. Its processing is described in Tenjin's Privacy Policy, and AppLovin describes its use of information in AppLovin's Privacy Policy.

3. Your choices

  • Consent. In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, the app requests your consent through Google's User Messaging Platform before any advert is requested or Tenjin is connected. Tenjin reads the same saved consent choice before connecting. If the required purpose is refused, Tenjin opts out and makes no attribution request. If you decline personalised advertising, adverts may still be shown but are not personalised. You may revisit your choice by clearing the app's data (Settings → Apps → Box Stack → Storage → Clear data).
  • Advertising identifier. You may reset or delete it at Settings → Privacy → Ads on your device.
  • Google ad settings. You may manage personalisation at adssettings.google.com.
  • Access or deletion. You may email us at the address in section 9 to request access to or deletion of attribution data. We may need your advertising identifier to locate the request with the provider.

4. Sharing you initiate

Two controls send something to another application, and only when you use them: sharing a score card offers that image, and sharing the app sends a link to its store listing. The receiving application is the one you select. We receive nothing.

5. Android backup

If Android's backup is enabled on your device, your records and settings may be copied to your own Google account backup and restored to a new device. This is performed by Android under your Google account and is covered by Google's Privacy Policy. The game in progress and the advertising counters are excluded from backup. We cannot access this data.

6. Children

The app is not directed to children under 13 and is not published under Google Play's Designed for Families programme. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

7. Deleting your data

Uninstalling the app removes everything stored locally. You may also clear it at Settings → Apps → Box Stack → Storage → Clear data. The advertising identifier belongs to your device rather than to the app; see section 3. Clearing or uninstalling the app does not delete data already transmitted to an advertising or attribution provider. Contact us using section 9 if you want us to forward an attribution-data request to Tenjin.

8. Changes to this policy

If a future version of the app collects information beyond what is described here, this policy will be updated before that version is released and the effective date above will change.
