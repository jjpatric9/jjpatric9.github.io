Privacy Policy — Box Stack

For Box Stack (com.boxstacker.app), developed and published by Tallman Games.

Effective 7 September 2026

This policy describes what the app stores, what its advertising provider collects, and the choices available to you.

The app has no account, no sign-in and no server of ours. Nothing you build, score or unlock is transmitted to us. It contains no analytics or crash-reporting software. Advertising is the only reason the app uses the network.

1. Information stored on your device

The app stores the following locally and does not transmit it:

  • your scores, records, badges, landmarks and experience total
  • lifetime counts of runs, rows, boxes and time played
  • your settings, and whether you have completed the tutorial
  • the game in progress
  • when an advert was last shown, and how many runs ago, so that adverts can be spaced

This information is not linked to your identity, and we have no means of accessing it.

2. Information collected for advertising

Adverts are supplied by Google AdMob. To serve them, Google collects and processes:

  • your device's advertising identifier, a resettable ID that does not contain your name, email address or telephone number
  • app activity: that an advert was requested, displayed or interacted with
  • device and network information inherent in making the request, including IP address

Google acts as an independent data controller for this information. Its use is governed by Google's Privacy Policy and by how Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services. We do not receive it, and Google provides us with no reports, identifiers or profiles.

Adverts appear as a banner, occasionally between runs, and optionally when you choose to watch one to continue a run. No advert is shown while a run is in progress.

3. Your choices

  • Consent. In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, the app requests your consent through Google's User Messaging Platform before any advert is requested. If you decline personalised advertising, adverts are still shown but are not personalised. You may revisit your choice by clearing the app's data (Settings → Apps → Box Stack → Storage → Clear data).
  • Advertising identifier. You may reset or delete it at Settings → Privacy → Ads on your device.
  • Google ad settings. You may manage personalisation at adssettings.google.com.

4. Sharing you initiate

Two controls send something to another application, and only when you use them: sharing a score card offers that image, and sharing the app sends a link to its store listing. The receiving application is the one you select. We receive nothing.

5. Android backup

If Android's backup is enabled on your device, your records and settings may be copied to your own Google account backup and restored to a new device. This is performed by Android under your Google account and is covered by Google's Privacy Policy. The game in progress and the advertising counters are excluded from backup. We cannot access this data.

6. Children

The app is not directed to children under 13 and is not published under Google Play's Designed for Families programme. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

7. Deleting your data

Uninstalling the app removes everything it has stored. You may also clear it at Settings → Apps → Box Stack → Storage → Clear data. The advertising identifier belongs to your device rather than to the app; see section 3.

8. Changes to this policy

If a future version of the app collects information beyond what is described here, this policy will be updated before that version is released and the effective date above will change.
